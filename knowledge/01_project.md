# GreenNest Residences - Project Knowledge Base (fictional demo company)

## Project overview
GreenNest Residences is a premium gated community in Whitefield, Bengaluru, built by GreenNest Developers. It has 4 towers with 14 floors each and 420 apartments spread over 6.5 acres, with 75 percent open green space. The project is designed for families and IT professionals who want a quiet home close to the tech parks.

## Configurations and pricing
Price, cost and rate of flats: 2 BHK apartments are 1,150 to 1,250 square feet and start at 85 lakh. 3 BHK apartments are 1,550 to 1,700 square feet and start at 1.2 crore. 3 BHK premium units with a study room are 1,900 square feet and start at 1.45 crore. Prices include car parking and clubhouse membership. GST and registration charges are extra. There are no 1 BHK units in this project.

## Payment plans and home loans
We offer a 20:80 payment plan: the buyer pays 20 percent of the price in instalments up to possession (starting with a 2 lakh booking amount) and 80 percent at possession, usually through a home loan. A construction-linked plan is also available. The 2 lakh booking amount is fully refundable within 15 days. Home loans are pre-approved by SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and Axis Bank, with interest rates starting around 8.4 percent, and banks usually fund up to 80 percent of the property value.

## Payment modes
The booking amount can be paid by UPI, net banking (NEFT or RTGS), debit card or cheque in favour of GreenNest Developers. Cash payments are not accepted, as per company policy and income tax rules. Every payment gets an official receipt by email and WhatsApp.

Our free loan advisor gives an exact quote during the site visit.

## Amenities
Amenities include a 40,000 square foot clubhouse, swimming pool, gym, indoor badminton courts, children's play area, jogging track, co-working space, EV charging points, 24 by 7 security with CCTV, power backup and rainwater harvesting. The project is IGBC Gold pre-certified as a green building.

Address: GreenNest Residences, Survey No. 42, Varthur Main Road, near Siddapura bus stop, Whitefield, Bengaluru 560066. Sales office open 10 AM to 6 PM, closed on Tuesdays. ITPL tech park is 10 minutes away, the Whitefield metro station is 8 minutes away and Kempegowda International Airport is about 60 minutes away. Towers A and B are ready to move in. Towers C and D will be handed over by December 2027. The project is RERA registered with Karnataka RERA, registration number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/DEMO. All approvals including BBMP plan sanction and environmental clearance are in place.

Free site visits are available every day except Tuesday, between 10 AM and 6 PM. A visit takes about 60 minutes and includes a model flat tour, clubhouse walk-through and a meeting with a home loan advisor. Free pick-up and drop is available within 10 kilometres of the project.

## Frequently asked questions
Maintenance charges are 4 rupees per square foot per month. Each 2 BHK gets one covered car park and each 3 BHK gets two. Pets are allowed and there is a dedicated pet park. NRIs can buy through a power of attorney and get dedicated NRI support. Resale and rental assistance is available through our partner agency.

## Handling price objections
If a customer feels the price is high, explain the value: car parking and clubhouse membership are included, the 20:80 plan keeps the upfront payment low, and Whitefield property prices have grown about 8 to 10 percent a year. Offer a site visit so they can see the quality, and mention the current month's free modular kitchen offer.
