Budget under 50 lakh: GreenNest Lakeview in Electronic City (1 BHK from 38 lakh, 2 BHK from 55 lakh). Budget 50 to 80 lakh: GreenNest Meadows in Sarjapur Road (2 BHK from 62 lakh) or GreenNest Lakeview 2 BHK. Budget 80 lakh to 1.2 crore: GreenNest Residences in Whitefield (2 BHK from 85 lakh). If a customer's budget is lower than the project they asked about, suggest the project that matches their budget and offer a site visit there.
